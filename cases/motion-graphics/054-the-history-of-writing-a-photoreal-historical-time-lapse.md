# The History of Writing, a photoreal historical time-lapse

Claude Fable 5.2 case 54 of 145 · Motion Graphics and Launch Videos · [简体中文](#中文)

By [@rileybrown](https://x.com/rileybrown) · [original post](https://x.com/rileybrown/status/2119946022767980985) · posted 2026-11-10 · [watch it in the apimodels.app gallery](https://apimodels.app/claude-fable-5-2-prompts/motion-graphics)

- **What it is:** "The History of Writing" is a 96-second historical short told in a photoreal time-lapse style.
- **Media:** Video, 96 s. About 47K views and 658 likes as of 11 November 2026.
- **Prompt:** published by the creator; at 546 characters it is excerpted below, with our outline of how the brief is built.
- **Note:** The prompt comes from the author's v2.video project page. Three follow-up prompts came after it: Make the music beautiful. / 4K / make the foliage really realistic and everything super realistic.

**Prompt (excerpt; full text at the [source](https://v2.video/riley/the-history-of-writing/v1))**

```text
I want you in a new thread to create a video using the same technique that created this video... But i want it to be historical. Pick a location / building / item in history and show it as a time lapse as it goes from age to age.
[…]
```

**How the brief is built (our outline)**

- Asks for a new thread that reuses "the same technique" as an earlier video, but on a historical subject.
- The model picks a location, building or item and shows it as a time lapse from age to age.
- The tone is set in words rather than numbers: deep and emotional, no humans needed, everything realistic, great music.
- The title is fixed as "The History of ___", with the subject left to the model's own taste.
- Three short follow-ups came after it (better music, 4K, more realistic foliage), listed in the note above.

To make one like it: the [Three.js starter](../../starter/threejs) turns a page with renderAt(t) into an MP4 (tested), and our [Claude Fable 5.2 prompting guide](https://apimodels.app/claude-fable-5-2-prompting-guide) has six brief templates.

**写实延时摄影风的写作历史短片**

作者：[@rileybrown](https://x.com/rileybrown) · [原帖](https://x.com/rileybrown/status/2119946022767980985) · 发布于 2026-11-10 · [在 apimodels.app 案例库看成片](https://apimodels.app/zh/claude-fable-5-2-prompts/motion-graphics)

- **作品：** "The History of Writing"：写实延时摄影风的历史短片，96 秒。
- **媒体：** 视频 96 秒。截至 2026-11-11 约 4.7 万 浏览、658 赞。
- **提示词：** 作者公开了原文，全文 546 个字符，上方只放节选，下面是我们整理的结构说明。
- **备注：** 提示词取自作者的 v2.video 项目页；之后还追加了三条：Make the music beautiful. / 4K / make the foliage really realistic and everything super realistic.

**这份提示词的结构（我们整理）**

- 要求开一个新对话，沿用之前某支视频的「同一种技术」，但换成历史题材。
- 由模型自己挑一个地点、建筑或物件，用延时摄影的方式展示它跨越一个个时代。
- 基调用文字而不是数字来定：要有深度、有情绪，可以没有人，所有东西都要极度写实，音乐要好。
- 片名固定为「The History of ___」，主题让模型按自己的兴趣选。
- 之后又追加了三条短指令（音乐更好、4K、植被更写实），见上方备注。
